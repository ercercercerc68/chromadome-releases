# ChromaDome (beta)

Paint the light dome of your Cinema 4D scene. Drag lights around a flat map of the sphere, and the dome follows live in Redshift, Arnold (C4DtoA) or the Standard renderer. Whatever you put on the map is what your chrome, glass and glossy surfaces reflect.

## Install

1. Download `ChromaDome_<version>.zip` from the [latest release](../../releases/latest).
2. Unzip it into your Cinema 4D plugins folder (Edit > Preferences > Open Preferences Folder > `plugins`).
3. Restart Cinema 4D and open Extensions > ChromaDome. Click **New shaderball** for a test scene, or **Attach / create rig** in your own scene.

## Updates

The panel checks this page once a day. When a newer version is out, an **Update to x.y.z** button appears under the map: it installs the new version, keeps the old one as a backup, and asks you to restart Cinema 4D.

Formerly called HDRI Studio.

## Requirements

- Cinema 4D 2026 (tested with 2026.4 on Windows)
- Redshift, Arnold (C4DtoA) or the Standard / Physical renderer. Octane support is untested.

This is a beta: feedback is very welcome.
